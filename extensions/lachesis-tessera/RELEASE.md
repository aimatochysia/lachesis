See whether pixel art tiles seamlessly: select an area and Tessera shows it repeated side by side, live, while you keep drawing. It only reads your sprite, so it never adds layers or undo steps. Brick (half-offset) repeat and drawing across the seams are coming next.

**Menu: File > Scripts > Tiled Preview**
- **Tiled Preview**: hold its shortcut to peek at the tiles over the canvas, or, with "Preview window" picked in the settings, open and close a live preview window.
- **Tiled Preview Settings...**: show as peek or window, repeat direction, number of tiles and zoom.
