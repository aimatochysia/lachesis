Quietly takes snapshots of your work while you draw, so you can turn them into a timelapse. A snapshot is taken only after you edit, is a lossless 1x PNG of the visible layers of the current frame, and is saved next to your project in `snapshots/<project name>/`.

**Menu: File > Scripts > Snapshots**
- **Snapshots Enabled**: turns snapshotting on or off (checkmark when on).
- **Snapshot Settings...**: idle delay, max wait and the per-snapshot size cap.
